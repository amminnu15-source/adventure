Our Origin

Born From a Shared Obsession

The story behind how Breakout came to life — from idea to reality.

🔑
2018
Year Founded

It started in a cramped apartment in 2017. Three friends — an architect, a game designer, and a theatre director — believed escape rooms could be more immersive.

"We didn't want to build puzzles. We wanted to build worlds you could walk into and lose yourself inside."

After months of prototyping and testing, Breakout opened its first room in Nellore in 2018 with one mission — create unforgettable experiences.

Today, we continue crafting original puzzles, real environments, and stories that challenge and excite every player.
