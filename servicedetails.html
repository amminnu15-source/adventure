Your Mission

How The Night Unfolds

Phase 01
The Entry Hall
You wake in a cold foyer. Gas lamps flicker. A locked iron door and a shattered portrait of Lord Ashford are all that greet you. Find the first key hidden within the broken frame.
Phase 02
The Library of Whispers
Ancient tomes hold encrypted clues. Decode the cipher hidden across three journals to reveal the ritual chamber's location — but don't disturb the raven.
Phase 03
The Séance Room
A spirit board, nine candles, and a message scratched into the floor. Piece together Ashford's final words to unlock the hidden passage.
Phase 04
The Ritual Chamber
Six artefacts. One binding seal. Assemble the ritual in the correct order within the final fifteen minutes — or remain forever.
