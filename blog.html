Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping into the shadows of ESCAPEQUEST.

What exactly is an escape room?

An escape room is a physical adventure game where players solve a series of puzzles and riddles using clues, hints, and strategy to complete the objective—usually "escaping" from the room—within a set time limit.

Are we actually locked inside the room?

For safety reasons, you are never truly "locked" in. While the door remains closed to maintain immersion, every room is equipped with an emergency exit button that allows you to leave at any moment if necessary.

What is the ideal team size?

Our rooms are optimized for squads of 2 to 6 agents. This ensures everyone has a role to play and the physical space remains comfortable for high-intensity coordination.

What happens if we get stuck on a puzzle?

Every squad is assigned a dedicated Game Master who monitors your progress via the Intel Terminal. You can request tactical hints at any time if your momentum stalls.

Is there an age requirement for agents?

Agents under 14 must be accompanied by an adult. Some mission nodes feature high-intensity themes (horror or psychological thrillers) that are recommended for ages 16 and up.

How early should we arrive for our mission?

We recommend arriving at least 15 minutes prior to your scheduled extraction time. This allows for your tactical briefing and equipment check before the clock starts.
